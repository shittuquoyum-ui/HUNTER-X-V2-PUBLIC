# Hunter X

**The bridge for Nigerian students to discover and pursue opportunities in Korea.**

Hunter X brings scholarships, university admissions, internships, jobs, exchanges, hackathons and conferences in Korea into one place, so students don't have to dig through dozens of university and government websites.

- **Discover** opportunities pulled from official sources
- **Save and track** the ones you're applying to
- **Get reminded** before deadlines close

👉 **Try it:** https://hunter-x-beta.vercel.app
